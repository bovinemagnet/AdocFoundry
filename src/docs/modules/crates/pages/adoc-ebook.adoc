= adoc-ebook
Paul Snow
:revnumber: 0.0.0
:description: The book model shared by the e-book exporters: a rendered view tree mapped to one book of chapters, links, a table of contents, metadata and embedded pictures.

`adoc-ebook` turns a rendered view tree into one book model that e-book writers write.
It is at layer L2, beside `adoc-render`, which it reads.
It began as the model inside xref:crates:adoc-kindle.adoc[adoc-kindle] and was moved out unchanged in behaviour, with neutral names, so a second exporter can share it.
`adoc-kindle` is its first user.
The EPUB exporter, xref:crates:adoc-epub.adoc[adoc-epub], now reads the book too.

== What it does

The crate is given a `Document`, which is an article (a `Page`) or a book (a `Plan` of `BookItem` values: parts, headings and pages), and `Options`, and builds one `Book` from it with `Book::build(&Document, &Options)`.
Each writer then writes that one book, so the formats it serves never differ in content.
It knows nothing of the engine, the workspace, a UI framework or any file format.

The book holds:

* chapters in reading order, with the ids `c1`, `c2` and so on, and their blocks and inlines;
* a nested table of contents;
* metadata: the title, authors, language, a stable `urn:uuid:` identifier derived from the title, authors and version (and no clock), the publisher, the description, the date (from `revdate`) and the version (from `revnumber`);
* assets, the embedded pictures.

Problems are reported as `Diagnostic` values, each with a `Severity` and a location at its source.

== Where it sits

`adoc-ebook` is headless.
It depends on `adoc-render`, an allowed same-layer pair listed in `SAME_LAYER_ALLOWED` in `xtask/src/layers.rs`, and on `adoc-core`, `adoc-dom`, `resvg` and `sha2`.
`adoc-kindle` and `adoc-epub` depend on it in turn, pairs listed there too.
It must never build `adoc-pdf`, `adoc-docx`, Typst or the engine.
It starts no process and uses no network: `cargo xtask layers` refuses `std::process`, `Command::new`, `std::net`, `TcpStream` and `UdpSocket` in its sources, with the same forbidden paths as `adoc-kindle`.

== Anchors and links

* Anchors are generated, never taken from a path: `c<n>-a<m>` for an id a page defines, `c<n>-n<number>` for a footnote and `c<n>-r<number>` for its first mark.
They are stable, and cannot collide across pages.
* A cross reference becomes a link inside the book when its target page is in the book.
Otherwise it is the page's published URL when a site URL is configured (`page_urls`), and failing that it stays text with a warning at its source.

== Pictures

Images, diagrams and formulas are embedded.
The `media` module reads each picture through the caller's `FileAccess`, and tells PNG, JPEG, GIF and SVG apart by their bytes, never by their names.

* Raster pictures are checked whole and kept as they are.
* SVG is drawn as a PNG in process with `resvg`, at twice its size on a white ground, and nothing is loaded from outside the file.
* Each file is kept once however often it is shown, and identical bytes are kept once.
* The limits are 16 MiB a file, 40 megapixels, and 256 MiB of pictures a book.
* A remote image is never fetched: it becomes a link to its address, with a warning.
* A picture that is missing, unreadable, malformed, oversized or out of bounds is its description, with a warning at its line.
